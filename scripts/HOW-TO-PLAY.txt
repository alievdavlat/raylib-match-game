raylib-match-game
=================

Run match-game.exe. No installation needed.

Controls
  Click a tile, then click a neighbouring tile to swap them.
  Line up 3 or more equal tiles to score.
  Esc quits.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time,
because the game is not code-signed. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Keep all files in this folder together: the game needs the two .dll files
and the resources folder next to match-game.exe.
